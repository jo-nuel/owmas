# ADR 0001: Preserve the registered primary OWMAS design

Date: 22 August 2026

Status: accepted

## Context

The Week 5 divergence audit found that the registered primary OWMAS signal,
with beta 0.50 and the neutral gamma profile, differs from its nearest SMA
benchmark on 4.92% of eligible days. The supervisor approved continuing and
left the choice among the three presented options to the student.

## Decision

Use Option 1. Preserve beta 0.50 and the neutral gamma profile as the primary
design. Report the 4.92% divergence as a limitation and substantive finding.
Do not tune the primary design after observing the audit.

## Consequences

- The original pre-registration remains the primary analysis.
- The divergence result must be disclosed when interpreting performance.
- The pre-registered beta 0.75 setting may appear only as sensitivity analysis,
  not as a replacement primary specification.
- Week 6 data verification and statistical inference may proceed.
