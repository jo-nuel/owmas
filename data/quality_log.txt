[2026-08-15] outlier investigation note (manual, not from verify_data.py)
Investigated the 3 dates exceeding 0.5% close-vs-close diff in the run above
(2009-07-16, 2015-03-12, 2015-03-13).

Open/High/Low/Volume agree closely between spy.csv and spy_stooq.csv on all
three dates (~0.01-0.05% typical diff, comparable to routine noise on
non-outlier days). This rules out a date-alignment artifact: a
shifted/mislabeled row would move the whole OHLCV bar, not Close alone.

Daily range (high-low) for context, since a narrow range would make "close
sits near an extreme" weak evidence by itself:
  2009-07-16  spy range=1.69 (1.82% of close)   stooq range=1.69 (1.79% of close)
              spy close at 17.2% of its own range; stooq close at 78.8%
  2015-03-12  spy range=1.98 (0.96% of close)   stooq range=2.31 (1.13% of close)
              spy close at 96.0% of its own range; stooq close at 14.9%
  2015-03-13  spy range=3.35 (1.63% of close)   stooq range=3.53 (1.70% of close)
              spy close at 37.3% of its own range; stooq close at 99.7%
None of these three ranges is unusually narrow for SPY (typical daily range
is roughly 1% of price), so the divergence is not simply an artifact of a
tight trading band. What the range positions show is that the two vendors
place Close asymmetrically within a similarly-sized range on the same day --
sometimes near opposite ends (2015-03-12), sometimes only one vendor sits at
an extreme while the other is unremarkable (2015-03-13, 2009-07-16).

This pattern is CONSISTENT WITH a vendor-level closing-print discrepancy
(e.g. primary auction close vs. a composite/consolidated close) -- that is a
hypothesis inferred from the price pattern, not confirmed against either
vendor's methodology documentation or a third source, and should be reported
as unresolved rather than explained. Caveat: 2009-07-16's 1.1% discrepancy is
large for that mechanism specifically -- routine primary-vs-composite close
differences are typically far smaller than a full percent of price, so this
magnitude is not confidently attributable to that cause alone.

Net: 3 of 5244 overlap days (0.057%) have an unresolved close-only
discrepancy >0.5%; OHLV on those same days and Close on all other days agree
to within a few basis points across the 20+ year overlap.
